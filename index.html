Frequently asked questions

Speed shows 0 or does not update
Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Speedara and choose “While Using the App”. Use the app outdoors with a clear view of the sky. Speeds below about 2 km/h are shown as 0.
Is the speed exactly the same as my vehicle’s speedometer?
No. GPS speed can differ by a small amount from your vehicle’s speedometer. Use Speedara as a guide only.
How do I change units or the speed limit alert?
Use the km/h / mph switch and the “Speed limit alert” settings on the main screen.
What is Demo mode?
Demo mode shows simulated speed so you can try the app without moving.
