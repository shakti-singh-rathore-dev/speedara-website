Speedara Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Speedara (“the app”) is a GPS speedometer for iPhone, published by Shakti Singh Rathore (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Location data

The app asks for your device’s precise location while you use it, only to calculate and display your speed, distance and trip time. Your location is processed on your device. We do not collect, store, upload or share your location with our servers, and we do not have an account system.

You can turn off location access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Speedara. The app will not show your speed without it.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves a few simple preferences on your device (for example, your speed-limit setting). This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain information, such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show ads and measure performance. If you are asked for permission to track, you can choose “Ask App Not to Track”, and you can limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Safety notice

Do not use or hold your phone while driving or riding. GPS speed can differ from your vehicle’s actual speedometer and may be inaccurate, especially in tunnels, dense areas or poor signal. Use the app as an aid only, and always follow local traffic laws.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with a new effective date.
